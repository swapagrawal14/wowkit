# Editorial launch
Best for: creative tools, independent studios, product launches.
1. Compact masthead: wordmark, 3 anchor links, primary CTA.
2. Oversized editorial headline and a specific one-sentence promise.
3. One original visual demonstrating the idea, not random stock imagery.
4. Three concrete capabilities. No fabricated metrics.
5. Interactive proof: real demo or clearly labeled concept.
6. A visible next step and restrained footer.
Use asymmetric space, a serif/sans contrast and one bright accent.
